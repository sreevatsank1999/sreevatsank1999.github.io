---
title: "PHYNet: A Gold-Standard Labelled PHY-Layer Dataset"
group: spectrum
order: 2
period: "2024 – 2026"
---
PHY-layer detectors and classifiers are hard to compare because there is no large, accurately labelled dataset of real over-the-air signals. PHYNet builds one. GNU Radio transmitters and hardware sniffers for Wi-Fi, BLE, Zigbee, LoRa, and 5G produce captures labelled from the sniffers' decodes, with tooling for iterative decoding and time-synchronizing the sniffers. The dataset also shows how little today's systems know about the spectrum around them.
