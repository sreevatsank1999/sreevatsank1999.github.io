A statistical approach to wideband spectrum sensing that learns each signal's arrival process from heavily undersampled observations, with only 1–5% real-time coverage, and uses the resulting model to direct a small number of radios. An optimal cooperative scheduling algorithm extends the approach to multiple receivers, improving prediction over the state of the art.
