title: "rfYOLO: Phase as a Modality for Signal Detection"
group: spectrum
order: 4
period: "2022 – 2024"
badges: ["IARPA-funded"]
---
Spectrogram-based detectors throw away phase. rfYOLO adds a phase transform as an extra input to YOLO-style detectors, which improves how they place signals in time and frequency and recognize their modulation.
