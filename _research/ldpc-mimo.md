Mission-critical communication demands extreme reliability with sub-millisecond round trips. This work began as a research internship at Télécom Paris and continued as my B.Tech. thesis at IIT Indore with Dr. Prabhat Kumar Upadhyay. I characterized the 5G NR LDPC base graphs across packet sizes. Real-time traffic such as H.264 video cannot wait to fill the large blocks where LDPC performs best, so it pays a reliability penalty. Accumulating packets before encoding wins much of that reliability back at the cost of delay, and I mapped out the resulting latency–reliability trade-off for video over a multi-cell MIMO system.
