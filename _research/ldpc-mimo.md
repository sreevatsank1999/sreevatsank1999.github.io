This work began as a research internship at Télécom Paris and continued as my B.Tech. thesis at IIT Indore with Dr. Prabhat Kumar Upadhyay. I characterized the 5G NR LDPC base graphs across packet sizes and showed that real-time traffic such as H.264 video, which cannot wait to fill large blocks, pays a reliability penalty. Accumulating packets before encoding recovers much of that reliability at the cost of delay, and I mapped the resulting trade-off for video over a multi-cell MIMO system.
