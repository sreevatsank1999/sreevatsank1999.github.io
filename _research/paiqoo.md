How good does the network have to be for a robot to succeed at its task? PAIQoO quantifies the network requirements of Physical AI applications through their *quality of outcome* (QoO): how end-to-end latency, sensing fidelity, and radio conditions together decide whether a task succeeds. Higher-fidelity sensing helps only until the extra data starts to delay the action, and a slightly worse action taken on time often beats a perfect one that arrives late. The goal is to turn task-level requirements into concrete network requirements. A network-aware sensing model can then choose what to sense, and at what fidelity, as conditions change.
